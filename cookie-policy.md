<h1>Cookie Policy</h1>

<p><strong>Last updated: October 3, 2026</strong></p>

<br /><br />

<h2>Quick Summary for Visitors</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend uses cookies and similar browser-storage technologies to
operate the Site, remember privacy preferences, and measure general website
usage.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Necessary technologies</strong> support essential Site functions
    and privacy preferences.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Google Analytics</strong> is used to understand general Site usage.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Country detection</strong> is performed using a Cloudflare-hosted
    service so the Site can determine which consent experience to provide.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Canada, the EU/EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and unknown locations</strong>
    are currently treated as consent-required regions.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Visitors in other countries</strong> may have Analytics enabled
    automatically under Beauty Bookend's current regional configuration.
  </li>

  <li>
    <strong>Advertising storage and personalized advertising remain disabled</strong>
    through the current Google consent configuration.
  </li>
</ul>

<p>
Beauty Bookend does not sell personal information.
</p>

<p>
For more information about how information is handled, please see our
<a href="https://beautybookend.github.io/BB/privacy/">
Privacy Policy
</a>.
</p>


<h2>1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?</h2>

<p>
Cookies are small text files that websites may place on a device to remember
information or support website functions.
</p>

<p>
Websites may also use similar technologies such as browser local storage,
scripts, tags, and other storage or access mechanisms.
</p>

<p>
For simplicity, this Cookie Policy sometimes uses the term "cookies" to
include cookies and similar technologies where appropriate.
</p>


<h2>2. Necessary Technologies</h2>

<p>
Necessary technologies support essential website functions and privacy
preferences.
</p>

<p>
Beauty Bookend's Silktide consent system uses browser local storage to
remember choices such as whether Analytics has been accepted or rejected.
</p>

<p>
These preference-storage mechanisms are used so that the Site does not need
to ask for the same choice on every page visit.
</p>

<p>
You may clear browser cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
Doing so may cause Beauty Bookend to request your preferences again.
</p>


<h2>3. Country Detection</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend uses a Cloudflare-hosted country-detection service to determine
the approximate country associated with a visitor's network connection.
</p>

<p>
The purpose of this country detection is to determine whether the Silktide
consent interface should be shown before Analytics storage is enabled.
</p>

<p>
The country-detection request may involve technical information, including
an IP address, being processed by Cloudflare as part of providing the service.
</p>

<p>
Beauty Bookend does not use this feature to determine a visitor's precise
physical location.
</p>


<h2>4. Regions Where the Consent Banner Is Used</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend currently requires an explicit Analytics choice for visitors
detected in:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Canada</li>
  <li>European Union member states</li>
  <li>Other European Economic Area countries</li>
  <li>The United Kingdom</li>
  <li>Switzerland</li>
  <li>Unknown or unrecognized locations</li>
</ul>

<p>
In these regions, Analytics storage begins in a denied state and remains
denied unless the visitor accepts Analytics.
</p>

<p>
For visitors detected in other countries, Beauty Bookend's current regional
configuration may enable Analytics automatically.
</p>


<h2>5. Silktide Consent Manager</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend uses the <strong>Silktide Consent Manager</strong> to manage
Analytics choices in consent-required regions.
</p>

<p>
Where displayed, the consent interface provides options to:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Accept Analytics</li>
  <li>Reject non-essential technologies</li>
  <li>Review and change preferences</li>
</ul>

<p>
The visitor's choice is stored in browser local storage.
</p>

<p>
The saved preference applies to Beauty Bookend in that browser profile.
It is not stored separately for each country.
</p>

<p>
For example, changing your VPN from one European country to another does not
automatically erase a previous Beauty Bookend Analytics choice.
</p>

<p>
If a saved preference exists, the full consent banner may not appear again
on a later visit even if your apparent country changes.
</p>


<h2>6. Google Analytics</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend uses <strong>Google Analytics 4</strong> to understand general
website usage and improve the Site.
</p>

<p>
Google Analytics may process information such as:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Pages viewed</li>
  <li>Visit and session information</li>
  <li>Browser and device information</li>
  <li>Referring pages</li>
  <li>Approximate geographic information</li>
  <li>Site interactions</li>
</ul>

<p>
Beauty Bookend does not use Google Analytics for the purpose of personally
identifying individual visitors.
</p>


<h2>7. Google Consent Mode</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend uses Google's <strong>Advanced Consent Mode</strong>.
</p>

<p>
When a page initially loads, Analytics storage is configured as denied.
The Google tag may nevertheless load.
</p>

<p>
When <code>analytics_storage</code> is denied, Google states that first-party
Analytics cookies are not read or written. Google may still receive limited
cookieless signals for measurement and modelling.
</p>

<p>
If Analytics consent is granted, the consent state is updated and Google
Analytics may use Analytics storage and receive normal measurement data.
</p>

<p>
For visitors in countries where Beauty Bookend does not require an explicit
Analytics choice under its current configuration, Analytics storage may be
updated to granted automatically.
</p>


<h2>8. Advertising Technologies</h2>

<p>
Beauty Bookend does not currently use the consent system to enable personalized
advertising.
</p>

<p>
The current Google configuration keeps the following permissions denied:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><code>ad_storage</code></li>
  <li><code>ad_user_data</code></li>
  <li><code>ad_personalization</code></li>
</ul>

<p>
If advertising or remarketing technologies are introduced later, this Cookie
Policy and the consent configuration may be revised.
</p>

<h2>10. What Happens If Analytics Is Rejected?</h2>

<p>
In a consent-required region, rejecting Analytics keeps
<code>analytics_storage</code> denied.
</p>

<p>
Google Analytics does not read or write first-party Analytics cookies while
Analytics storage is denied.
</p>

<p>
Because Beauty Bookend uses Advanced Consent Mode, Google may still receive
limited cookieless signals for measurement and modelling purposes.
</p>

<p>
Rejecting Analytics does not prevent you from reading normal Beauty Bookend
content.
</p>


<h2>11. What Happens If Analytics Is Accepted?</h2>

<p>
If you accept Analytics, Beauty Bookend updates
<code>analytics_storage</code> to granted.
</p>

<p>
Google Analytics can then use Analytics storage and process normal measurement
information according to Google's policies and Beauty Bookend's Analytics
configuration.
</p>


<h2>12. Managing Your Preferences</h2>

<p>
Where the Silktide consent manager is available, you can use its preferences
control to review or change your Analytics choice.
</p>

<p>
You can also delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
</p>

<p>
Deleting the saved Silktide preferences may cause Beauty Bookend to treat you
as a new visitor and request a new consent choice where applicable.
</p>


<h2>13. Changing Countries or Using a VPN</h2>

<p>
The consent choice stored by Silktide is associated with Beauty Bookend and
your browser profile. It is not stored as a separate choice for every country.
</p>

<p>
If you make a consent choice while connected from one country and later use
the same browser while connected through another country or VPN location,
the previously saved Beauty Bookend preference may still apply.
</p>

<p>
Clearing Beauty Bookend's local storage removes the stored Silktide preference
and allows the consent process to be tested again as a new visit.
</p>

<p>
Relevant third-party services may include:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>GitHub Pages</strong> — hosting</li>
  <li><strong>Cloudflare</strong> — country detection</li>
  <li><strong>Google Analytics</strong> — Site measurement and analytics</li>
</ul>

<p>
These providers have their own privacy policies, terms, and data-processing
practices.
</p>

<p>
Privacy rights depend on your location and the laws applicable to the Site
and the relevant processing.
</p>

<p>
Depending on the circumstances, rights may include access, correction,
deletion, withdrawal of consent, objection, restriction, portability, or
complaint rights.
</p>

<p>
For additional information, see the
<a href="https://beautybookend.github.io/BB/privacy/">
Privacy Policy

</p>

Beauty Bookend may update this Cookie Policy when technologies, service

</p>

<p>
The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
</p>
